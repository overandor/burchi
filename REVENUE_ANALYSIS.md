# PART 1: PLATFORM REVENUE MODELS

## 1.1 Revenue Model Classification

Every platform in the massage/escort ecosystem uses one of these revenue models:

```
Model A: Therapist-Pays (Listing/Subscription)
  └── Therapist pays monthly fee for profile listing
  └── Platform revenue = therapist count × monthly fee
  └── Examples: rent.men, rentmasseur, masseurfinder, masseurmatch

Model B: Freemium (Free listing + Premium upsell)
  └── Basic profile free, premium features paid
  └── Platform revenue = premium subscribers × monthly fee
  └── Examples: massageanywhere (free), rubratings (affordable)

Model C: Client-Pays (Client membership)
  └── Clients pay to contact therapists / view premium content
  └── Platform revenue = client subscribers × monthly fee
  └── Examples: rent.men (client premium $19.95/mo)

Model D: Commission (Per-booking cut)
  └── Platform takes percentage of each booking
  └── Platform revenue = total bookings × commission %
  └── Examples: MassageBook (percentage-based)

Model E: Featured Placement (Pay-per-day)
  └── Therapists pay for top placement ("Man of the Day")
  └── Platform revenue = featured slots × daily rate
  └── Examples: rent.men "RentMan of the Day", rentmen.eu "Video of the Day"
```
